Privacy

XEOTRN is a product of XEO Group, Woonona NSW. This page explains what we collect through this website. It is a plain-English summary, not legal advice, and we'll update it before the app launches.

What we collect

When you join the waitlist we collect your first name, email, age group and the optional details you choose to give (position, club, state, what you want to try). If you join through a friend's invite link, we record who invited you so they can move up the queue.

How we use it

To manage the waitlist and early access, to tell you about XEOTRN, and to understand who the app is for. We do not sell your information.

Analytics and ads

We may use privacy-friendly analytics and the Meta (Facebook and Instagram) pixel to measure how people find the site and how our ads perform. These may set cookies or similar identifiers.

Where it's stored

Waitlist details are stored with our database provider (Supabase). Emails about new signups are sent to our team inbox.

Young people

XEOTRN is for players aged 14 and over. Parents and coaches are welcome on the waitlist. If you're under 16, please ask a parent or guardian before signing up.

Your choices

Email Xeotrn@xeosports.com to see, correct or delete your details, or to unsubscribe.
