FAQ

Questions

When does it launch?

Soon. Join the waitlist and you'll hear first, with early access.

Do I need a coach or a team?

No. XEOTRN is built for solo training. You need a ball, some space and a few cones. A wall or rebound board helps for some steps.

Does my position change what I train?

Every player follows the same pathways. Your position shapes REX's advice and your Position DNA.

Who is it for?

Players aged 14 and over, from grassroots to NPL. Parents and coaches are welcome to join the waitlist too.

How much does it cost?

Start with a 7-day free trial, then $14.99 AUD per month.

Who's behind it?

XEOTRN is built by XEO Group in Woonona, NSW, home of XEO Sports teamwear and the XEO HQ indoor football facility.
